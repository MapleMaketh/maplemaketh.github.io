What to include

  • Product name and version. Include the package version you are using.
  • Your environment. Exact Unity version, operating system and render pipeline.
  • Reproduction steps. A short sequence we can follow.
  • Expected and actual results. Explain what changed or stopped working.
  • A minimal example. A small anonymized CSV that preserves the fixed schema and reproduces the issue.
  • Relevant error text or a cropped product UI screenshot. Remove private information first.
Share the problem, not private data.

Do not send customer data, license keys, tokens, authentication details or your entire project. Remove personal details and local paths from logs and screenshots. Do not upload these materials to public GitHub Issues.

Before you send

Check the quick start and verified environment and limitations. If recovery is blocked, preserve the backup and describe the reported conflict rather than deleting the journal.
